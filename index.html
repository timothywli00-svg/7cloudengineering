Design apps

Design apps

Apps on this site are for exploring options and criteria. They are not permit drawings. A result is a starting point for a scoped design, or a way to see why a concept fails, not a document you can submit.

How a design is chosen

How a design is chosen

  1. 01
    Site constraints. Soils, water table, setbacks, slopes, existing improvements, and what the survey and logs actually show.
  2. 02
    The rule that applies. The health-department, drainage, or water-quality criteria for that jurisdiction, not a generic template.
  3. 03
    Feasible options. The system types or drainage concepts the site and the rule still allow, and the ones they remove.
  4. 04
    One recommended concept. A single concept you can react to, chosen under a written scope.
  5. 05
    Plans and calculations. The drawings, sizing basis, and narrative a reviewer can check.

An app, when one is live, may help with the first three steps. Steps four and five stay with the responsible professional and a written scope.

We will not recommend a system type from a photo and a guess at the soils.
